# STATUS v0.4.0-beta

**Release type:** public mechanics demo / playtest build  
**Play the game:** https://5ritzky.github.io/fullstack-app/status-beta/  
**Playtest instructions:** [PLAYTEST.md](PLAYTEST.md)

## Included in this build

- Browser-playable story arc set in Meridian Group.
- Four NPC profiles.
- R/S/P indicators separated from relationship values and status.
- Relationship and obligation state.
- Social event and consequence log.
- Eight status concepts represented in the model.
- RU / EN / DE / ES language switching.
- Undo, save export and import controls.
- Local tester feedback form with copy/download report options.
- A defined ending for the current story arc.

## Testing focus

This build is intended to gather evidence on four product-readiness criteria:

1. Whether cooperation, independence, confrontation and alliances lead to meaningfully different consequences.
2. Whether relationships and obligations visibly influence later scenes.
3. Whether social transitions are reachable and supported by verifiable social facts, rather than only R/S/P growth.
4. Whether the current arc has a satisfying ending without relying on promised future content.

## Known limitation — status rules

The current status-transition rules are experimental and should be treated as a testable hypothesis. They are not a replacement for the approved v0.3 model. Reports that identify unsupported, unreachable or contradictory transitions are especially valuable.

## Data and privacy

The game page states that it uses no cookies, advertising or server-side event uploads. Saves and feedback reports are handled locally in the browser. Local saves may be cleared by the browser. Test reports are not sent automatically; testers must copy or download and submit them manually.

## Not a final commercial release

This build is for controlled public testing. It does not establish that the social-status model, balance, localization or commercial criteria have passed acceptance testing.
